# Data-based decision + one-week plan (written 2026-10-17, Saturday)

**Evidence:** YouTube Data API v3, 120-day window, India/Hindi. Raw: `planner/data/outliers-*.json`; analysis with relevance filter: `planner/data/analysis-*.md` (regenerate: `node planner/yt-outliers.mjs …` then `node planner/yt-analyze.mjs …`).
**How to read it:** search returns the *most-viewed* videos in the window per query (survivorship bias: shows what worked, not how many tried and failed; competition is NOT measured). "views/sub" is the outlier signal (small channel, big views). Views may include promoted/live traffic. Correlation, not causation. Demand for event topics is seasonal: queries for Dhanteras/Diwali look weak *now* because they have not peaked yet.

**Angle from the data:** origin questions win ("Maa Durga ka janm kaise hua?"). So the film asks how the goddess was born, not just who killed whom. Risk: high competition and seasonal; window closes about 20 Oct.
