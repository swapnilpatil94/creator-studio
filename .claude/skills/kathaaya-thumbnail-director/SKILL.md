# KATHAAYA thumbnail director
Owner-authored brief, kept here as the working rules. Our studio has no image generator: the owner generates the artwork externally from the prompt this skill produces; the studio ingests it (`node tools/thumbnail.mjs stories/<name> --external <file>`), converts it to 1080x1920, runs the checks and shows it in the dashboard. If accurate Devanagari cannot be ensured by the generator, ask for text-free space and give a typography overlay spec; never ship garbled text. Never claim a design guarantees clicks.

## Permanent brand identity (never redesign per story)
Cinematic Indian ink storytelling on warm aged parchment (manuscript texture, subtle grain); expressive black ink brushwork, layered 2.5D depth; restrained crimson and antique gold; dramatic chiaroscuro and divine illumination; bold authentic Devanagari readable at small size; brand KATHAAYA, tagline "Ancient Stories. Reimagined Through Ink."; ancient, mysterious, reverent, dramatic, premium. No photoreal AI gloss, glossy 3D, cartoons or random neon.

## 1. Analyse the story first
Central mystery/revelation/conflict/turning point; the single most compelling moment communicable in one image (chosen from the actual story, not the flashiest scene); what the audience knows vs wants to discover; ONE primary hook: Mystery (what happened?), Revelation (what was hidden?), Conflict (who prevails?), Transformation (how did this happen?), Consequence (what changed?), Awe (what extraordinary power?). Thumbnail and title must complement, not repeat. Preserve the dignity of deities and the source tradition.

## 2. Psychology (deliberate)
One dominant focal point (face/gesture/light guides the eye). One clear visual question (information gap without confusion). Three-level hierarchy: primary = main face/event; secondary = conflict or revealing detail; tertiary = short Hindi headline + subtle brand mark. Strong contrast (ink on parchment, gold on black, selective crimson; brightest light on the key element). Mobile-first: thick Devanagari strokes, generous spacing, clean silhouette, no paragraphs. Emotional tension fitting the story (no forced horror in a sacred or peaceful tale). Curiosity without deception: never invent events or promise revelations absent from the video.

## 3. Composition and technical rules
9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 preferred. Keep the main face, essential text and key details off the extreme edges and the Shorts interface areas (bottom ~20%, right edge). One dominant headline plus at most one short supporting line; headline about 3-7 natural Hindi words. No tiny text, no stray logos/watermarks (remove generator marks), no decorative frames or particle clutter. Separate foreground/middle/background; must read at a small preview. Landscape videos get a separate 16:9 composition, not a crop.

## 4. Creative development
Internally explore three genuinely different concepts: A mystery-driven, B emotion-driven, C conflict/revelation-driven. Score each: clarity, curiosity gap, emotional impact, mobile readability, originality within the brand, faithfulness to the story, complementarity with the title. Choose one; do not merge into clutter. One standalone image, no collage.

## 5. Image prompt must specify
Exact narrative moment and traditionally correct characters; appearance, expressions, gestures, clothing, symbolic attributes; camera angle and hierarchy; foreground/middle/background; light direction, shadows, atmosphere, colour balance; parchment + hand-drawn ink + controlled red/gold; the exact Hindi headline and its placement; mobile readability/safe areas; explicit exclusions (extra limbs beyond the iconography, malformed hands, wrong sacred attributes, gibberish lettering, duplicate faces, random English text, clutter, unrelated symbols).

## 6. Deliverables per story (`stories/<name>/THUMBNAIL_BRIEF.md`)
1 story diagnosis; 2 three concepts (one sentence each); 3 final selection (design judgment vs proven performance); 4 headline (+ optional support); 5 complementary title; 6 final image prompt; 7 final thumbnail (generated externally; ingest); 8 QC checklist (readability, brand, hierarchy, story accuracy, sacred iconography, safe placement, text correctness); 9 A/B test recommendation (different hooks, judged on impressions, CTR and watch time/retention together, only with enough traffic).

## Non-negotiable
Do not redesign the brand per story or repeat one composition/headline formula mechanically. Do not choose art because it is merely beautiful. No sensational claims beyond the narration. Traditional iconography is not interchangeable fantasy decoration. A thumbnail is not "successful" without performance evidence.
