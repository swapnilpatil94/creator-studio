# Abhimanyu and the Chakravyuha — fact notes
Traditional account: Mahabharata, Drona Parva (13th day of the Kurukshetra war).
- Drona forms the chakravyuha; only Arjuna among the Pandavas is said to know how to break it. Arjuna is drawn away by the Samshaptakas. (Drona Parva)
- Abhimanyu tells Yudhishthira he knows how to enter the formation but not how to leave it. (Drona Parva)
- Number of rings (seven) and which maharathis surround him vary across tellings — narration says "several maharathis", no names, no counts of attackers.
- The popular "learned in the womb" detail is a traditional retelling, not narrated.
- Final line is an authored reflection, not a scriptural quotation.
