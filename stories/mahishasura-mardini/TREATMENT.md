## Lens (storytelling §3: invert the villain / find the asset in the failure)
Obvious lens: "Durga kills Mahishasura". Chosen lens: **the goddess is made out of the gods' defeat.** The failure of every god is the raw material of the goddess. Interpretation (labelled): the moral "they were stronger together"; the fact it rests on (traditional account): the gods' combined energies form her.

## Last dab first (storytelling §2) — loops into the hook

## Structure choice
Candidates: single journey (Mahishasura's rise), before/after (gods then goddess), **a list that turns (eight gods give, one goddess exists)**. Chosen: the turn from eight separate lights into one figure, because it is the lens made visible.
