## Lens (storytelling §3: invert the villain / find the asset in the failure)
Obvious lens: "Durga kills Mahishasura". Chosen lens: **the goddess is made out of the gods' defeat.** The failure of every god is the raw material of the goddess. Interpretation (labelled): the moral "they were stronger together"; the fact it rests on (traditional account): the gods' combined energies form her.

## Last dab first (storytelling §2) — loops into the hook
Last line: "देवता अकेले हारे थे। साथ मिले, तो देवी बन गए।" (9 words; repeats the hook's "देवताओं की हार")

## Hook: five options, formula named (hook-formulas), best chosen
0. **CHOSEN — direct address + origin question (#21/#19/#9): "तुम जानते हो, तीनों लोकों के देवता हारे तो दुर्गा बनीं कैसे?" (hook-lint 75/100: specificity 80, address 100, stakes 85, curiosity 90)**
1. Origin + question (#19/#9): "तीनों लोकों के देवता हार चुके थे… तब जन्मीं देवी दुर्गा। कैसे?"
2. Contrarian/impossible claim (#4/#17): "देवी दुर्गा को किसी ने जन्म नहीं दिया। वो बनीं… देवताओं की हार से." (lint 39/100: no viewer address, no question; its "no mother" payload moved into m04)
3. Direct address + stakes (#21): "जब तुम्हारे सारे देवता हार जाएँ, तब क्या बचता है?"
4. Reveal (#5): "अब देखो, देवी दुर्गा कैसे बनीं।"
5. Statistic (#1): rejected — no verified number exists in our ledger.

## Benchmark (grammar only)

## Structure choice
Candidates: single journey (Mahishasura's rise), before/after (gods then goddess), **a list that turns (eight gods give, one goddess exists)**. Chosen: the turn from eight separate lights into one figure, because it is the lens made visible.

## Signature shot / native move
The eight sigils converge into one point and burst into the goddess (dots reforming). Peak at ~75%: the trident strike, preceded by silence.

## Visual method (revised 2026-10-17): real paintings as a coloured dot matrix
The dot-silhouette goddess was dropped (unreadable). Public-domain paintings of Durga (credits in `assets/CREDITS.md`) are baked into a 16-colour palette and drawn as coloured dots through the shared camera: the viewer sees the actual god, in the channel's dot-cinema style, with dot-dissolve cuts between paintings. Callouts (dotted ring + Devanagari label) name the painted parts on the narration word. Thumbnails use the painting in full colour (the real god, as the channel's Ganesh thumbnails do).
