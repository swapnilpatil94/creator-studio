# Thumbnail brief: Durga's birth (written under `kathaaya-thumbnail-director`)

## 1. Story diagnosis
- **Central question (transformation):** how did Durga come to exist? Traditional account: she was not born of a mother; the defeated gods' combined energy (tejas) became her. Other texts differ (e.g. Katyayani), so the thumbnail asks, it does not assert.
- **Emotional trigger:** awe + curiosity ("what was she made from?").
- **Strongest single moment:** the gods' light streaming into one point and the goddess forming, not the killing (everyone expects the killing; the origin is the gap).
- **Audience knows:** Durga defeats Mahishasura. **Wants to learn:** how she came to be.

## 2. Three concepts
- **A Mystery:** the goddess only as a crowned silhouette inside a blinding point of light, with the question as the headline.
- **B Emotion:** extreme close-up of the goddess's eyes opening in radiance, a single gold tear of light, gods tiny and kneeling behind.
- **C Transformation/conflict:** the defeated gods in prayer on the left, their light streams converging into the rising goddess on the right, the demon's horned shadow looming below.

## 3. Final selection
**C.** It shows the unusual fact (gods' light becomes the goddess) and the stakes (the looming enemy) in one readable diagonal: gods -> light -> goddess. A is cleaner but anonymous; B is emotional but hides the story. This is design judgment, not performance evidence.

## 4. Headline
**"माँ दुर्गा का जन्म?"** (4 words) + ONE support line: **"महिषासुर वध की कथा"**. Remove the third text block ("देवताओं के तेज से कैसे प्रकट हुई"): the owner's brief allows one headline and one short supporting line.

## 5. Title pairing (complementary, does not repeat the thumbnail's question)
**जब देवता हारे, तब दुर्गा बनीं… बिना किसी माँ के 🔱 | KATHAAYA** (the title answers the thumbnail's "how?" with the gods' defeat and "no mother", as the traditional account; the description notes that other texts differ).

## 6. Final image prompt (for the owner's image tool)
> Vertical 9:16 YouTube Shorts artwork, 1080x1920, in the KATHAAYA style: cinematic Indian ink storytelling on warm aged parchment with visible manuscript texture and subtle paper grain; expressive hand-drawn black ink brushwork, layered 2.5D depth, dramatic chiaroscuro; restrained crimson red and antique gold only.
> Moment: the gods' energy becomes the goddess. Right two-thirds, upper: Goddess Durga emerging in golden divine light, an 8-armed iconographic figure with a gold crown, steady fierce but dignified expression looking toward the viewer, red sari, a trident and a discus held in raised hands, a halo of fine gold line-work. Left, middle: four crowned/ascetic gods (Shiva with trident, Vishnu, Brahma white-bearded, Indra) kneeling or standing with folded hands, faces lit by the light, in traditional dress. Thin streams of gold light flow from each god's hands and chest across the centre and converge into the goddess (the visual spine: gods -> light -> goddess). Lower third: the large horned silhouette of the buffalo-headed demon Mahishasura looming in ink-black shadow with faint crimson eyes, NOT yet defeated, kept low and dark so the goddess stays the brightest element. Background: distant temple spires in sepia ink wash, sparse ink splatters, a few crimson brush strokes.
> Hierarchy: 1 the goddess's face and crown; 2 the converging light and the demon's shadow; 3 text. Brightest light on the goddess's face and the convergence point. Keep the face and text away from the extreme edges and out of the bottom 20% and the right edge (Shorts interface).
> Text (exact Devanagari, thick brush lettering, high contrast, mobile-readable): headline "माँ दुर्गा का जन्म?" placed upper-left on a dark ink-splash with "माँ दुर्गा" in antique gold and "का जन्म" in white with a crimson question mark; ONE small crimson brush-stroke ribbon beneath: "महिषासुर वध की कथा". No other text. If the tool cannot spell Devanagari, leave clean text-free space upper-left and the studio will overlay the type.
> Exclude: extra limbs beyond the intended eight arms, malformed hands, wrong sacred attributes, gibberish lettering, duplicate faces, random English text, watermarks, signatures, logos, frames, photorealism, glossy 3D, neon colours, clutter, a dead or dismembered demon, gore.

## 9. Testing recommendation
Shorts autoplay in the feed does not show the thumbnail; it matters on the channel page, search, suggested and browse surfaces, so impressions will be limited at first. When there is enough traffic (YouTube's Test & compare is for long-form), test: A = this transformation art vs B = concept A (mystery silhouette) with different headline hooks, and judge impressions, click-through rate and average percentage viewed together (a higher CTR with lower retention is a failure). With the current 31-201 views per video there is not enough data to conclude anything yet.
