# Hanuman's leap — fact notes
Traditional account: Valmiki Ramayana, Sundara Kanda (and Kishkindha Kanda for the Jambavan reminder).
- Vanara army halts at the southern shore; Jambavan reminds Hanuman of his forgotten strength. (Traditional: a childhood curse made him forget until reminded — tellings differ; not narrated.)
- Hanuman grows vast and leaps from Mahendra mountain.
- Mainaka rises from the sea offering rest; Hanuman touches it and declines. (Sea-god's request in the tradition.)
- Other episodes (Surasa, Simhika) omitted for length; regional tellings vary in detail.
