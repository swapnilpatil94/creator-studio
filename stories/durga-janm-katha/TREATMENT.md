## Packaging first (locked before the script)
- Title: **देवता सब हार गए, तब क्या हुआ? दुर्गा के जन्म की पूरी कथा | देवी महात्म्य | KATHAAYA** (10 options in `publish.seed.json`).
- Thumbnail: concept A, cracked "१०० साल की हार" + the goddess's eyes opening; brief in `THUMBNAIL_BRIEF.md`. Art is generated by the owner.
- Target viewer (one sentence): a Hindi-speaking devotee or curious viewer around Navratri who knows Durga kills Mahishasura but has never heard how she came to exist or what the gods actually gave her.
- Series: part 1 of the Devi Mahatmya middle story. Part 2 (Shumbha, Nishumbha, Raktabija) is teased in the last 20 seconds.

## Lens and the last dab
- Lens: **united weakness becomes strength**. Every chapter shows an individual power that fails alone and works pooled.
- Last dab (written first, in `beats.json`): "देवता अकेले हारे थे। साथ मिले, तो देवी बन गए।" followed by the king's loop: the question King Suratha asked in chapter 1 is answered by the end.

Starts mid-battle (the goddess lifting the cup, "गरज ले, मूर्ख"), then the exact question the title and thumbnail ask ("सौ साल की हार के बाद दुर्गा का जन्म कैसे हुआ?"), the promise (what each god gave, how many forms the demon took, and the vow at the end). Hook lint 75/100, 0 fails (`planner/hook-lint.mjs`). The first frames show the goddess, matching the thumbnail's promise.

## Climax (75-90%) and resolution
Silence (declared in `sound.json`) before "सिर कट गया।" then the shockwave and 3.6 s of breath; the vow follows. Resolution is the last dab, three notes about variants (arms count, Bhadrakali name, Durga named in chapter 11, the boon from other texts is not in the text we read), and a single CTA (the tease).

## Truthfulness (mythology gate)
`claims.json` (124 claims) holds the ledger; verification state is PARTIAL: claims come from a chapter-level read of an English transliteration of the Devi Mahatmya (archive.org), plus commentary and Wikipedia for cross-checks, not a verse-level check. Contested details are narrated as variants (arms, the god-to-body-part mapping was removed). Interpretation is labelled "व्याख्या" on screen. Pictures are Indian miniature paintings (credits in `assets/CREDITS.md`); where no public art exists for the king, the merchant and the hermitage, the film uses dotted scenes, flagged in `ASSET_PLAN.md`.

## Visual method (how a 10-minute film exists with 21 pictures)
Reuse factor 3 shots per picture, but each reuse has a different focus (auto-picked high-detail regions), a different move (push, pan, close) and a different dot treatment; paintings alternate with code-drawn symbols, diagrams, atmosphere and Devanagari typography every 3-8 s (`ASSET_PLAN.md`: 65 painting shots, 66 code-drawn).

## Sound
Procedural bed with a mood per chapter (dread, calm, dread, wonder, siege, siege, release, wonder), bells on gifts, riser into the light mountain, impact and crack at the kill, 2 declared silences. `sound.build.mjs` regenerates `sound.json` from the narration timings.
